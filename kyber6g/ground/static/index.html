DETECTION SNAPSHOTS

ENCRYPTED RECORDINGS (.k6grec · ML-KEM-1024 wrapped key · decrypt on GCS only)

◼ SAVE seals the recording on the Pi's SD card; it is then fetched and decrypted here by itself. The list shows every recording on the card, newest first: one that is not here yet has its FETCH & DECRYPT button.

SEALED AUDIO (.k6gaud · ML-KEM-1024 + X25519 wrap · ML-DSA-87 signed · a key per block)

No microphone is attached to the Pi: a file in its inbox stands in for one (kyber6g.sh audio-put FILE). It is cut into its frames and sealed block by block; the same sealed blocks go to the SD card and, with LIVE, onto the link. Blocks lost on the air are fetched again from the card and checked against the root the UAV signed. The Pi cannot open its own clips.
